# 主角动作原画

这三张透明 PNG 是 v0.3 的原始美术资产。Codex 使用内置 ImageGen，以项目的 01 待机和 03／04／05 剑击概念姿势为参考。新动作统一由右上方受光，阴面保留墨色。第一张新原画作为后两张的角色参考。

从仓库根目录执行 `python tools/rig/prepare_hero.py`，再执行 `python tools/rig/make_rig.py tools/rig/hero_rig_def.py game/assets/rig/hero`。前者按各姿势的地平线切出游戏帧，后者将新待机分解为九个蒙皮部件。需要 Pillow、NumPy、SciPy、OpenCV。开发分支已包含处理后的游戏纹理。

## 主角待机、拔剑和横斩过渡

> Create a production TRANSPARENT RGBA character animation atlas on a wide landscape canvas, exactly THREE separate isolated, uncropped full body paintings of the SAME Taoist swordsman side by side, one figure per equal-width column. No labels, no border, no ground/shadows, no paper or text, no FX or energy swoosh. This art goes into a 2D side-scrolling game at 150 pixels tall. Reference 1 specifies identity, clothing, colours, hair, equipment and precise resting leg and arm placement. Reference 2 specifies brushwork and fighting posture. Authentic Chinese painted game illustration, confident tapered ink brush edges, large simplified ochre robe folds, rectangular mustard-paper talisman, orange gourd and a single long straight iron jian, weathered dark indigo trousers, reddish brown boot wraps. Restrained warm ivory light from upper right sculpts bony shoulder, ribcage, forward knuckles and weighted thigh; deep blue-grey shadows under far sleeve and skirt. Face readable at a small scale. Use broad hand-painted planes, a narrow dark edge only on the shadow side, clean separation at wrist, distinguish silhouette of both hands, no artificial rim, halo, black sticker stroke or glowy outline. FULL BOOTS and sword in frame, each figure centered in its own cell with generous true transparent gutters, equal implied standing stature, same floor line. Column 1: standing ready with face looking RIGHT, in exactly the reference 1 front-three-quarter narrow stance. Sword held with lowered RIGHT screen-side hand at hip, blade slants to lower right side, left screen-side hand holding waist gourd. Boots apart facing to the right, knees unlocked; chest gently turned toward right. Column 2: his sword drawn back before a horizontal strike towards the RIGHT; weight on rear leg (screen left), hips turn away and front shoulder foreshortened, feet planted apart. The sword points upward to the rear left, rear elbow bent, sword-bearing hand near shoulder. Column 3: the next in-between pose during this horizontal stroke, facing RIGHT, rotate hips and chest forward, transfer weight toward front/right boot, right sword hand leads across ribs, long blade angles upward forward right. Keep clean open space between blade and face. All 3 poses consist ONLY of this young male Taoist, subtle brush texture, strong illustrated sculptural form, no scenery.
